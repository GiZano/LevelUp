# 🏔️ LevelUp v1.0.0 - The Alpine Release

Welcome to the very first official release of **LevelUp**! 
After a long journey of prototyping and refining, we are finally ready to drop v1.0.0. This app was born from a simple need: eliminating *decision fatigue* in our free time and building the scaffolding to actually achieve our long-term goals.

LevelUp turns your goals into mountains to climb, breaking them down into manageable camps and weekly blocks. 

## ✨ What's included in v1.0.0

### 🎯 Core Gamification
- **Peaks & Camps**: Define your long-term goals (Peaks) and break them into actionable sub-goals (Camps). 
- **Visual Progress**: Watch your SVG mountain fill up as you conquer your camps, with a satisfying visual celebration when you plant your flag at the summit.
- **Streaks**: Keep the momentum alive with a daily streak counter. Don't break the chain!

### 📅 Weekly Time-Blocking Planner
- **Zero Decision Fatigue**: A powerful but simple weekly grid (Morning, Afternoon, Evening) to drag and drop your tasks.
- **Activity Blocks**: Create reusable templates for your activities with specific time durations (e.g., "Study React", "Gym", "Reading").
- **Smart Categories**: Group blocks into categories, set weekly target hours, and track your progress in real-time. 

### 🎨 The "Alpine" UI/UX Redesign
- **Beautiful & Calm**: A completely custom "Alpine Dusk" theme featuring deep teals and warm sunset tones, designed to be easy on the eyes.
- **Native Polish**: Smooth layout animations when toggling blocks and interacting with lists.
- **Vector Graphics**: Say goodbye to OS-dependent emojis! We've integrated `@expo/vector-icons` and a sleek `IconPicker` for a professional, cohesive look across all devices.
- **Custom Typography**: Powered by the modern `Outfit` Google Font.

### 💾 Safe & Local-First
- **100% Privacy**: All your data lives on your device. No sneaky analytics, no cloud lock-in.
- **Backup & Restore**: Easily export all your Peaks, Categories, and Blocks to a JSON file to save on Google Drive, and import it back anytime.
- **Bilingual**: Fully translated in both English and Italian.
